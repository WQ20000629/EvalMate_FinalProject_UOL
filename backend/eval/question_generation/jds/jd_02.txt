Strong content creation skills with experience developing multi-format content and familiarity with relevant content creation and editing tools

Strong interpersonal and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with internal teams and external partners

Data-driven, creative and proactive, with strong attention to detail and a results-oriented mindset

Adaptable and resourceful, with the ability to thrive in a fast-paced and dynamic environment
