About the role

This is a fully remote position based in Singapore that reports directly to US headquarters. As a Customer Service Representative for a leading US-based specialty chemical manufacturing company, you will serve as the primary point of contact for customers and manage a range of customer-facing activities in a global environment.

Key responsibilities

Serve as the primary point of contact for customers via phone, email, and other communication channels
Manage customer accounts, including account creation, maintenance, updates, and account-related inquiries
Handle customer enquiries, complaints, and service issues, ensuring timely and effective resolution
Process orders, cancellations, returns, refunds, exchanges, and account adjustments accurately
Maintain detailed records of customer interactions, transactions, and account activities within CRM systems
Provide accurate information on products, services, pricing, warranties, promotions, and company policies
Identify customer needs and recommend suitable products or solutions to enhance customer satisfaction
Generate and qualify sales leads while supporting sales initiatives and customer retention efforts
Follow up on customer requests and complaints to ensure complete resolution and a positive customer experience
Collaborate closely with internal stakeholders and the US-based management team to ensure service excellence
About you

Proven experience in Customer Service, Customer Support, Sales Support, or a related role
Excellent customer-centric mindset with excellent problem-solving and conflict-resolution skills
Demonstrated ability to manage high volumes of customer interactions while maintaining service quality
Good communication, interpersonal, and active listening skills
Experience using CRM systems and customer management tools
Ability to multitask, prioritize workload, and work effectively under pressure
Detail-oriented especially on documentation and organizational skills
Positive attitude, adaptability, and ability to build excellent customer relationships
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and general computer systems
Diploma or Degree in Business Administration, Customer Service, or a related discipline
